# 90-day outcome simulation: order agent vs fixed reminders

> Simulation with synthetic patients and stated assumptions; not a clinical trial.

**Orders fulfilled within 90 days: 50.6 % with fixed reminders → 74.6 % with the order agent (+24.0 points (+18.5 to +28.6), 95 % interval over 20 seeded runs).**

Config `simulation.v1`, report `simulation-report.v1`. 400 synthetic patients per arm, orders placed over the first 30 days, observed until day 90; seeds 1–20. Every value is the mean over seeds with the 95 % interval across seeds in parentheses. "Fulfilled" is the engine's own status of the order at the end: the simulation never marks an order done. Method and assumptions: docs/simulation.md. Reproduce with `pnpm simulate`.

## Economics (assumption: price per consultation)

At 200 patients with new orders per month and USD 40 per consultation: 26.2 (15.2–38.8) more attended consultations per month, about USD 1,047 (USD 607–USD 1,553) per month. Only attended consultations count; lab and imaging revenue is not included.

- Most proactive messages to any patient in any 7 days: 2 (cap 2).
- Agent actions in the control arm: 0.

## Personas

- **fast_responder** (30.0 %): Replies to the first message and takes the first slot that fits. ASSUMPTION: the largest group. Most patients who read a message from their own clinic answer it, and many of them would have done the order anyway (high self-completion).
- **second_try** (15.0 %): Misses the first message, answers a later one. ASSUMPTION: busy patients who read messages late; a second contact several days later is what gets an answer.
- **slot_friction** (12.0 %): Answers, but the offered time never suits them ("not Tuesday", only Saturdays). ASSUMPTION: working patients for whom weekday mornings do not work; they book when the time changes, and rarely find a time on their own after a generic reminder.
- **wrong_number** (6.0 %): The phone on file is wrong: messages are undelivered until reception fixes it. ASSUMPTION: outdated or mistyped numbers. Once the right number is found they behave like an average patient.
- **reads_never_answers** (10.0 %): Reads messages but never answers them; a phone call from reception works. ASSUMPTION: patients who do not write back to a clinic but pick up the phone.
- **never_answers** (12.0 %): Never answers a message or a call. ASSUMPTION: disengaged patients. Nothing in either arm reaches them; they complete an order only on their own.
- **opts_out** (3.0 %): Asks the clinic to stop writing at the first reply. ASSUMPTION: a small share of patients do not want messages; both arms stop writing to them.
- **elsewhere_photo** (12.0 %): Did the test at an outside lab; sends a photo of the result when asked. ASSUMPTION: patients who use a cheaper or closer lab. The clinic never receives that result unless someone asks for it; asked, most send a photo.

## Assumptions

- ASSUMPTION: the persona mix and every probability in this file are plausible values chosen by the team, not measurements, and no study is cited for them. A clinic's own control group (V2 PRD, workstream B) replaces them with measured outcomes.
- ASSUMPTION: without any contact, a share of patients completes each order on their own (from 10 % to 60 % by persona); urgent visits half as often, because the patient does not know the result is abnormal. The same draw applies in both arms.
- ASSUMPTION: the control arm is usual care with fixed reminders: one generic message when the order is due and one 7 days later, no booking help, no follow-up calls, no way to send a result back. A patient who engages books or goes to the lab on their own (with the persona's probability).
- ASSUMPTION: reception completes 90 % of the agent's call tasks on their due day; 80 % of the patients who answer a call book on it. Clinic capacity is not a constraint: every patient sees the synthetic schedule's free slots.
- ASSUMPTION: every result is normal, so a result never opens a new abnormal follow-up during the window.
- SIMPLIFICATION: the patient's side of the conversation is the persona model, not a language model. The agent's policy is the real code: the ladder, the urgent and undelivered tracks, the contact cap (2 proactive messages per 7 days) and the slots of packages/core/src/engine/order-agent.
- SIMPLIFICATION: the agent takes a test or an urgent visit at once, and a control visit 14 days before it is due (the order agent has no start rule of its own yet).
- AS IN THE AGENT: once an order is booked the agent stops chasing it, even when the patient does not show up; a no-show is not recovered by the agent.
- ENGINE RULE: a visit order closes on a booking made in time and opens again when the booking date passes with no visit. "Fulfilled" is the engine's own status of the order at day 90.
- ECONOMICS: recovered revenue counts only attended consultations (agent minus control) at the configured price, USD 40 by default; lab and imaging revenue is not counted.
